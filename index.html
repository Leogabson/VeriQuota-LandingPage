The Problem

Sound familiar?

Carrier invoices tell you how much data was billed, but never explain why it ran out prematurely.

01

Data gone with little phone use

You bought a multi-gigabyte bundle in the morning. By afternoon, your carrier sends an exhaustion warning, even though your screen was turned off for most of the day.

02

Carrier dashboards lack granularity

Standard provider apps present single aggregate totals or delayed logs. They cannot tell you which specific package or background process initiated the transfer.

03

Zero recourse for unexplained bills

When billed usage does not match your real activity, customer service offers no dispute mechanism without independent, timestamped on-device records.

Architecture

How VeriQuota works

Three simple steps to establish reliable, independent accounting on your Android device.

1

Install and grant one local permission

Grant standard Android Usage Access during setup. VeriQuota reads internal socket statistics directly from the operating system without routing traffic through a remote VPN.

2

See real usage per app in a live breakdown

Review byte-level precision across foreground and background tasks. Identify misbehaving background synchronizations and silent updates in real time.

3

Get alerted before you are surprised

Set custom thresholds for high consumption or runaway usage rates. VeriQuota alerts you immediately when an application exceeds expected transfer volume.

Guaranteed Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

A monitoring tool should never become a tracker. VeriQuota is architected around strict local execution and verifiable boundaries.

Measured locally always

Network counters are read and stored exclusively in a sandboxed local database on your phone. No external servers participate in measurement.

Nothing uploaded by default

The application requires no account registration to function. Your logs, usage patterns, and app lists remain private to your hardware.

No ads, trackers, or data-selling

There are no third-party ad networks, telemetry bundles, or analyticsSDKs in our core binaries. Our incentives are aligned with user transparency.

Long-term Direction

An independent record for telecommunications

Beyond individual device insights, our future goal is to assemble an independent, entirely opt-in dataset comparing measured usage against billed usage across mobile networks. As an anonymized public record, this can provide concrete evidence for consumer advocates, investigative journalists, and regulatory oversight looking into unexpected billing practices.
